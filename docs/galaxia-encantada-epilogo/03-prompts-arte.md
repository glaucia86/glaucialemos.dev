# Prompts de arte — GE-PL-08, GE-GL-08 e GE-DOG-08

> **Modo de uso:** gerar três fontes separadas (fundo e duas spritesheets), inspecionar e aprovar, então integrar ao pipeline existente. Anexar as imagens-mestre `GE-MASTER-GL.png`, `GE-MASTER-DOG.png` e uma placa do universo aprovado para orientar o estilo. As duas capturas de *Project Hail Mary* fornecidas pela usuária podem servir de **moodboard de sensação** (escala e vibração de cores), nunca de composição a reproduzir. Toda a arte final deve ser original e apropriada para distribuição pública.

## A. Fundo: GE-PL-08-beyond-orbit.png

**Formato:** 16:9, idealmente 3840 × 2160 como fonte de alta resolução, sem texto, sem personagens, sem logos. O projeto já gera WebP otimizado para a aplicação.

```text
Create an ORIGINAL, production-ready, cinematic 2D PIXEL ART BACKGROUND PLATE for a premium software engineer portfolio universe titled “Galáxia Encantada”. Scene 08: “Beyond the Last Orbit” / “The Veil of Possibilities”.

A nearly silent observation terrace floats in deep interstellar space, attached to a tiny, wholly original research outpost whose geometry is unlike any recognizable film spacecraft. A narrow, firm horizontal standing deck occupies the LOWER RIGHT, with room for two small character sprites to be composited later, viewed from behind. No characters, animals, footprints, silhouettes, or embedded shadows from characters in this background. Give the platform physically readable depth and a subtle warm rim light; keep architecture minimal so the universe feels impossibly large.

The main subject is an IMMENSE, UNIQUE COSMIC NEBULA — “The Veil of Possibilities” — stretching across the middle distance and upper RIGHT TWO-THIRDS. It is NOT a planet or a circular sphere. Its silhouette is an original, asymmetric branching sculpture of translucent gas filaments, folded luminous sheets, coral-red dust and distant golden stars. Deep emerald and translucent mint currents run along one side, turning to warm amber, dusty copper, and luminous coral sparks as the ribbons disperse into space. Thousands of minuscule, unevenly distributed points of starlight create depth; tiny varied clusters drift visually through red-pink nebular regions. The brightest region is off-center around (72%, 43%), not behind the text. A few delicate faraway stars populate the quiet far-left side.

IMPORTANT COMPOSITION:
- Wide 16:9 with premium cinematic scale.
- Upper and middle RIGHT: monumental luminous filaments; no direct copy of any film frame.
- LEFT 35–40%: calm dark negative space to support readable website copy.
- LOWER RIGHT: firm wide platform, unobstructed space for small Glaucia and Prince rear-view sprites standing side by side (Glaucia around 70% width, Prince around 79% width, feet around 85% height when composited).
- Keep the distant nebula visually prominent but not so saturated that it burns out all stars.
- Suggest extremely subtle gravitational haze and cosmic dust, never smoky fog obscuring the actors.

ART DIRECTION:
Elegant fine-grained hand-authored pixel art with crisp microscopic pixel clusters, layered atmospheric depth and controlled luminous dithering only where artistically necessary. Sophisticated lighting, deep shadows, extremely detailed distant stars. Pixel art must remain recognizably PIXEL ART, not a smooth oil painting, blurry illustration, AI-smoothed photo or voxel scene. Preserve the fine 1–2 pixel visual density of the approved Galáxia Encantada backgrounds; NOT chunky retro 8-bit scaling. Final background plate should retain its original pixel detail; no forced downsampling to coarse blocks.

PALETTE:
abyss navy #060A18; dark aubergine #1B142D; deep emerald #226B59; aurora mint #83EFAC; soft amber #E9A966; coral #F07F77; distant rose #C75B76; gentle luminous ivory #FFF0C9.

ATMOSPHERE: intimate companionship confronted with infinite scale; wonder, curiosity, calm, acceptance, the promise of an unwritten next chapter. It must feel like the quiet epilogue of an ORIGINAL spacefaring adventure.

ABSOLUTE EXCLUSIONS:
No logos, no letters, no words, no UI, no HUD, no watermark, no movie shot recreation, no recognizable spacecraft, no franchise costumes, no lightsabers, no Star Wars motifs, no Petrova Line rendering, no giant spherical green-orange planet, no copied cinematic framing, no photorealism, no real persons or dogs embedded in the plate, no cropped stage.
```

## B. Glaucia: GE-GL-08-beyond-orbit.png

**Formato:** folha 2×2 de 4 poses, transparência real, fundo totalmente transparente, escala constante, pessoa de costas nos quatro quadros. Se a ferramenta não produzir transparência confiável, usar fundo de cor uniforme apropriado para recorte aprovado — jamais fundo estrelado na sprite sheet.

```text
Create a production-ready ORIGINAL 2D PIXEL ART ACTOR SPRITE SHEET of GLAUCIA for “Beyond the Last Orbit”, using the ATTACHED APPROVED GE-MASTER-GL character reference as the mandatory identity and pixel-art style baseline.

FOUR exactly equal full-body sprite panels arranged TWO ROWS x TWO COLUMNS. The same adult woman in all four panels, identical height, body silhouette, skin complexion where visible, hair color, hair length, recognizable hairstyle and proportions from the master. She is ALWAYS SEEN FROM THE BACK, gazing away from the viewer into an enormous cosmic scene. Keep both feet aligned to an identical ground baseline with consistent scale and shadow conventions. No front face, no 3/4 camera, no side-profile drift.

COSTUME: original interstellar explorer attire unique to Galáxia Encantada, elegant indigo-violet fitted exploration coat with fine teal seam lights and soft coral rim accents; practical matching boots; subtle original life-support or observation gear, no recognizable commercial or film spacesuit. Her hair remains identifiable from behind. Refined silhouettes, subtle fabric folds, restrained equipment detail.

POSES in reading order:
1. IDLE-BACK: standing upright, shoulders relaxed, hands at sides, contemplating infinity.
2. LOOK-UP-BACK: minimal upward head tilt, tiny shoulder shift; same foot anchors.
3. REACH-BACK: slowly raising one arm slightly toward the distant nebula, understated and emotionally restrained.
4. RETURN-BACK: arm partly lowered, body posture returning toward idle for a smooth loop.

ART: production-quality crisp fine-grained 2D pixel art matching the source master's exact density and 1–2 pixel edge language. Consistent sprite proportions, restrained highlight palette. No baked background, no text, no speech bubbles, no props not worn by the character. Transparent background with clean silhouette suitable for automatic pose extraction. Four isolated characters with generous separation; no overlap, cropping or loose artifacts. The outfit and animation must be ORIGINAL and NOT derived from recognizable movie silhouettes.
```

## C. Prince: GE-DOG-08-beyond-orbit.png

**Formato:** folha 2×2 de 4 poses, transparência real, fundo transparente, sempre visto de costas, patas alinhadas.

```text
Create a production-ready ORIGINAL 2D PIXEL ART DOG SPRITE SHEET of PRINCE, using the ATTACHED APPROVED GE-MASTER-DOG reference. He is the SAME small cream Pomeranian dog across every pose: identical fluff volume, pointed ears, cream fur, plume tail, short legs and cheerful proportions. He is always SEEN FROM BEHIND, beside Glaucia, looking out at a gigantic nebula; show NO human in this sheet.

FOUR equally-sized full-body panels in TWO ROWS x TWO COLUMNS, same body scale, ears and paws. Each pose keeps the paws on the SAME GROUND BASELINE and identical anchor. Preserve recognizability from behind via fluffy coat, ear tips and plume tail. Costume: a tasteful ORIGINAL violet-and-indigo small explorer jacket with mint seam accents, lightweight safety collar and practical mini boots, visually consistent with Glaucia’s outfit; do not use a pet accessory recognizably copied from any film.

POSES in reading order:
1. STAND-BACK: standing still and gazing forward, tail lifted.
2. LOOK-UP-BACK: lifting head slightly toward the sky, same footprint.
3. WAG-A-BACK: small happy tail motion toward camera-left; no paw shift.
4. WAG-B-BACK: complementary happy tail motion toward camera-right; exact same footprint.

ART STYLE: precise, fine crisp 2D PIXEL ART matching the approved Galáxia Encantada master, not cartoon vector art, not blocky oversized pixels, not photorealism. Clean sprite silhouette on genuine transparent background. Identical scale and fur colors in all panels, clear space between panels, no text, no borders, no baked shadows or nebula, no accessories changing between frames.
```

## Revisão visual antes da integração

- Fundo não contém Glaucia, Prince nem textos.
- Sprites mostram ambos realmente **de costas**, com mesma identidade aprovada.
- Arte não lembra cena exata ou geometria distintiva do filme.
- Plate `16:9`, sprites separáveis com alpha, baseline idêntico e dimensões regulares.
- O contraste permite copy legível na esquerda.
- Personagens ficam pequenos diante da vastidão, mas distinguíveis em 390 px de largura.
- Versões sem partículas e sem movimento continuam bonitas.
